📜 Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how DrakoFit collects, uses and protects information when you use the application.

📍 Location Permission

DrakoFit uses your device's location exclusively to record walking or running routes while an activity is in progress.

Your location is never sold or shared with third parties.

📱 Stored Information

The application stores information locally on your device, including:

  • 🐉 Dragon progress
  • ⭐ Experience points
  • 🏃 Completed activities
  • 📏 Distance travelled
  • 🔥 Calories
  • ⏱ Activity duration
  • 🗺 Route history

This information remains on your device unless you decide to remove the application.

📢 Advertising

DrakoFit may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google may collect advertising identifiers and other technical information according to its own Privacy Policy.

For more information, please visit:

Google Privacy Policy

🔒 Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information stored by the application.

No personal account is required to use DrakoFit.

👶 Children's Privacy

DrakoFit is not intended to collect personal information from children.
